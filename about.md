# Threat intelligence with a defensive focus

This site is a personal cybersecurity research publication focused on understanding real-world threats and translating technical findings into useful context for defenders.

My research interests include threat intelligence, malware analysis, ransomware campaigns, emerging adversary techniques, attacker infrastructure, and detection engineering.

## Research interests

- **Cyber Threat Intelligence:** tracking campaigns, tools, infrastructure, and changes in adversary operations.
- **Malware analysis:** investigating behavior, execution chains, persistence, and data collection.
- **Ransomware operations:** following RaaS ecosystems, affiliate activity, and operational developments.
- **Phishing and credential theft:** analyzing delivery methods, automation frameworks, and account-compromise techniques.
- **Detection engineering:** identifying behaviors and artifacts that can inform defensive monitoring and detection logic.
- **MITRE ATT&CK:** mapping observed techniques to a shared behavioral framework.

## Research approach

Research combines technical analysis with contextual intelligence from public reporting, security research, and relevant threat sources. Findings are assessed for practical defensive value and, where applicable, translated into detection ideas or actionable recommendations.

## Scope and responsible disclosure

Content is published for security research, awareness, and defensive purposes. Indicators and technical details should be validated against the context in which they are used. Attribution and actor claims are treated cautiously and should be understood in light of the evidence cited in each publication.
