# About

Welcome to my cybersecurity research blog.

I focus on threat intelligence, malware analysis,
ransomware campaigns, emerging adversary techniques,
and detection engineering.

The goal of this blog is to document technical findings,
analyze attacker behavior, and share actionable insights
that help defenders improve detection and response.

## Research Interests

- Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)
- Malware and infrastructure analysis
- Ransomware and financially motivated threats
- Detection rules and defensive engineering
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping
